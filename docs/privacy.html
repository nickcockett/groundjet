Privacy

How GROUNDJET handles the information you send us. Last updated 28 August 2026.

GROUNDJET is an Atlanta executive ground transportation service. This page explains what we collect when you request a journey or email us, and how that information is used.

What we collect

When you submit a journey request or write to hello@groundjets.com, we receive the details you provide: name, email, phone, travel date, pick-up and drop-off locations, passenger count, type of service, and any additional notes such as flight numbers.

Our website is static. Form submissions are sent by your own email client to hello@groundjets.com. We do not run advertising pixels or sell lists.

How we use it

We use your details to quote, confirm, and operate the journey you asked for, and to follow up on that request. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep request and booking correspondence as long as needed to provide the service, meet record-keeping needs, and resolve questions about a journey.

Sharing

We may share trip details with the chauffeur and licensed operator assigned to your journey, and with an FBO, hotel, or venue when coordination is required. We do not share your information for unrelated marketing.

Your requests

To access, correct, or delete personal information we hold, email hello@groundjets.com.
